# 🧬 The Science of Human DNA

## What is DNA?
DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) is the molecule that carries the genetic instructions needed for the growth, development, and functioning of all living organisms.

## Interesting Facts
- DNA is found mainly in the nucleus of human cells.
- It contains four nitrogenous bases: Adenine (A), Thymine (T), Cytosine (C), and Guanine (G).
- DNA has a double-helix structure.
- Almost every cell in the human body contains the same DNA.

## Why is DNA Important?
DNA determines many inherited characteristics and provides instructions for making proteins that help our bodies function.

## Did You Know?
If the DNA in one human cell were stretched out, it would be approximately 2 meters long!
